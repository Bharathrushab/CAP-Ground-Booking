1. Tournament Format & Structure

Overview

  • 150 registered players are drafted into 10 teams.
  • Each team is a 15-player squad: 1 captain + 14 drafted players.
  • The 10 teams are divided into 2 pools of 5 — Pool A and Pool B.
  • All games are 20 overs per side, 11-a-side.

League Stage — Cross-Pool Format

  • Each team plays all 5 teams from the opposite pool — 5 league games per team.
  • Teams do not play the other teams within their own pool during the league stage.
  • Total league games: 25 (each Pool A team vs each Pool B team, once).
  • Points: Win = 2 pts, Loss = 0 pts, Washout = 1 pt each, Tie = 1 pt each.
  • Standings within each pool are ranked by points, then Net Run Rate (NRR).

Knockout Qualification

  • The top 2 teams from each pool qualify for the semi-finals: A1, A2, B1, B2.
🏆 Semi-Final Bracket (Intra-Pool):

Semi-Final 1: A1 vs A2  (Pool A winner vs Pool A runner-up)
Semi-Final 2: B1 vs B2  (Pool B winner vs Pool B runner-up)
Final: Winner of SF1 vs Winner of SF2

Because every team played only the opposite pool during the league stage, pool-mates never faced each other. The semi-finals therefore pit same-pool teams against one another for a fresh matchup, while the final is a cross-pool decider.

2. The Player Draft

Draft Overview

  • 150 players register for the draft pool.
  • 10 captains are pre-designated by CAP (captains are not part of the draftable pool).
  • Each captain selects 14 players to complete a 15-player squad (captain + 14).
  • 10 squads × 15 players = 150 players — every registered player is drafted.

Draft Procedure

  1. CAP publishes the full list of registered players before the draft, with any available stats or grade.
  2. Captains draft in a snake order (1→10, then 10→1, and so on) to keep selection fair across rounds.
  3. The draft runs for 14 rounds — one pick per captain per round.
  4. A player, once picked, is removed from the pool and cannot be selected by another captain.
  5. Draft order for Round 1 is decided by a random draw conducted by CAP.
⚠️ Squads are locked after the draft. No trades, swaps, or additions are permitted once the draft concludes, except for the one-time backup-player replacement described below.

Backup Players & Replacement Draft

Registration is locked at 150 players. Any players who register beyond that cap are held as backup players in a reserve pool and are not initially drafted to any squad.

  • If a drafted player withdraws or leaves before the tournament, the affected team may replace them from the backup pool.
  • The replacement draft is held 2 days before the start of the tournament — a single consolidated window, not on a rolling basis.
  • If multiple teams are affected, replacement picks are made in order of original draft position: the team that had the 1st overall pick chooses their backup replacement first, followed by the 2nd, and so on.
  • Each affected team selects one backup player per lost player to restore their squad to 15.
⚠️ This replacement process happens only ONCE per team, at the single window 2 days before the tournament starts. After that, squads are final — no further replacements for any reason.

Pool Assignment

  • After the draft, the 10 teams are split into Pool A (5 teams) and Pool B (5 teams) by a CAP-conducted draw.
  • Pools and the full fixture list are published before the first game.

⏱️ Innings Time Limit: Each innings must be completed within the allotted window. Umpires and captains are responsible for maintaining the over rate and ensuring the innings finishes on time.

Minimum Players & Toss

  • A team must have a minimum of 7 players on the ground to start the game.
  • Toss time is 10 minutes before the scheduled start. Playing XI must be updated in CricClubs before the toss.
  • If a team does not have 7 players for the toss, they forfeit the toss. If both teams don't have enough, the first team to reach 7 wins the toss.
  • No additional players allowed after 30 minutes from game start. No excuses.

Late Arrival Penalties

Example: If the game is scheduled at 10:00 AM, the team must have 7 players on the ground and be ready to start.

10:10 AM (10 min late): Team loses 1 over.
Every 5 minutes after that: 1 additional over deducted (10:15 = 2 overs, 10:20 = 3 overs, etc.).
30 minutes late: If team still does not have minimum players, game is forfeited.

⚠️ Umpires MUST enforce this rule strictly. One delayed game must NOT affect the next scheduled game.

4. Player Eligibility & Registration

Squad Eligibility

Only players drafted to a team's 15-player squad are eligible to play for that team. A player may not appear for any team other than the one that drafted them.

Minimum Games for Playoffs

A player must have played a minimum of 3 games during the league stage to be eligible for knockout matches (semi-finals and final).

Player Identity & Photos

  • Every player must have a clear profile picture uploaded in the CricClubs app. The face must be clearly recognizable.
  • Umpires are authorized to check player identity using a photo ID during any game, especially during playoffs.
  • Playing an unregistered or non-squad player will result in the game being forfeited and possible disciplinary action.

Waiver Form

  • A waiver form is required for all under-18 players. The waiver must be signed by a parent or guardian before the start of the game.
  • Under-18 players without a signed waiver are not eligible to play.

No Transfers

No player transfers are allowed during the tournament. A drafted player stays with their team for the entire season.

5. Game Rules

Match Format

General Rules

  • A new ball shall be used at the start of each innings.
  • Each team must bring at least 1 new ball to their game.
  • LBW is applicable — standard ICC LBW rules apply.
  • Leg-bye runs are allowed as per ICC rules.
  • Batters may use batting gloves.
  • 10-minute break between innings.
  • Optional 5-minute mid-innings break if required, at the fielding side's discretion.

Wide Ball

  • Offside: If any part of the ball is inside the wide line, it is not a wide. The ball must be entirely outside the wide guideline to be called wide.
  • Leg-side: Any ball passing outside the leg stumps is a wide.
  • If a batsman moves across the stumps before the ball is delivered, the wide line moves with the batsman — as per ICC rules.
  • Above the head: A ball that bounces and goes above head height is called a wide (not a no ball).

No Ball

  • Full toss above waist height = no ball.
  • Bouncers: Only 1 bouncer per over is allowed. A bouncer is any delivery that rises above shoulder height of the batsman. The first bouncer above shoulder height in an over is a warning. The second bouncer above shoulder height in the same over is a no ball.
  • All foot-fault no balls apply as per ICC rules.
All no balls result in a free hit for the next delivery.

Other Rules

  • Run Out / Stumping — No Bails: Since this format uses stumps without bails, the fielder must hit the stumps with the ball to effect a run out or stumping. If the stumps have already been broken, the fielder must uproot a stump from the ground while holding the ball to complete the dismissal.
  • Retired Hurt: A genuinely injured batsman may retire hurt and return later when fit, with umpire and opposition captain approval.
  • Retired Out: A batsman who voluntarily retires (not injured) is retired out and cannot return.
  • No substitute runners are allowed under any circumstances.

🛑 Mandatory Fielding Restrictions (strictly enforced):

During the Powerplay (overs 1–6): No fewer than 9 players must be inside the 30-yard circle — i.e. a maximum of 2 fielders are permitted outside the circle.

After the Powerplay (overs 7–20): A minimum of 4 fielders must be inside the 30-yard circle at the moment of delivery. This is a must — a maximum of 5 fielders may be outside the circle.
  • Fielding positions are checked at the instant the ball is delivered.
  • If the fielding restriction is breached, the umpire shall call and signal a no ball.
  • If overs are reduced, follow the percentage breakdown and round to the nearest whole number.

8. Delays & Rain Management

Non-Weather Delays

  • 10 minutes after scheduled start: 1 over deducted from the late team.
  • Every 5 minutes after that: 1 additional over deducted.
  • If a game exceeds its window, teams may come back and finish on a rescheduled day.
  • If delays are cascading, umpires and captains must be firm in enforcing the time limit to protect subsequent games.

Weather / Rain Delays

  • Captains and umpires will decide on playing conditions during rain.
  • If both teams cannot play at least 8 overs each, the game is a washout and will be replayed entirely on the rescheduled day.
  • If rain interrupts after at least half of the second innings has been completed, the game will continue from where it was left off on the rescheduled day.
  • If a rescheduled game is also washed out, it becomes split points (1 point each).

Playoffs — Rain Rules

  • All semi-final and final games must have a result.
  • If a semi-final or final is not possible within the scheduled window, CAP reserves the right to reschedule the match.

Semi-finals are intra-pool (pool-mates who did not meet during the league stage). The final is a cross-pool decider between the two pool champions of the knockout.

Tied Knockout Games — Super Over (ICC Rules)

  1. The team batting second in the main match bats first in the Super Over.
  2. Each side nominates 3 batsmen. Loss of 2 wickets ends the Super Over.
  3. A player can bat and bowl in the Super Over — as per ICC rules.
  4. If the Super Over is tied, another Super Over is played until a result is achieved.

10. Cricket Bat Specifications

⚠️ Captains must carry the bat gauge provided by CAP. Any bat that does not pass through the gauge is not allowed in match play.
  • Overall length: max 38 inches (including handle).
  • Blade width: max 4.25 inches.
  • Blade depth: max 2.64 inches.
  • Blade edges: max 1.56 inches.
  • If a non-compliant bat is noticed mid-game, it must be replaced immediately. The umpire or any player should report it to CAP.

11. Bowling & Fielding Rules

Over Rate

  • Captains are responsible for maintaining a healthy over rate to ensure game completion within the scheduled time window.

Chucking (Illegal Bowling Action)

  • Defined by ICC: elbow extension exceeding 15 degrees.
  • Batsman may raise concerns with the main umpire, who may call a no ball if convinced.
  • If a bowler repeatedly chucks, the opposing team should record video evidence and submit it to CAP for review.

Fielding Absence Penalty

  • If a fielder leaves the field of play during the innings, they are penalized for the same number of overs they were absent before they may bowl or bat, as tracked by the umpires. This does not apply to injuries sustained during play and approved by the umpire.

12. Equipment & Stumps Rule

Each team must carry in their kit bag:

  • Stumps / wickets — one set per team (bails are not required)
  • Bat gauge (provided by CAP)
  • New ball (minimum 1 new ball per game)

Stumps Requirement — Progressive Penalties

🏏 Every team is responsible for bringing a set of stumps to their game. If a team fails to bring stumps, the following progressive penalties apply across the season:

14. Ground Logistics

CAP grounds have permanent pitches and boundary markings. There is no home or away team policy — all teams share equal responsibility for the facilities.

Ground Care

  • Set up wickets before the game. Remove them after the last game of the day.
  • Walk around the ground and surrounding areas after the game to ensure no trash is left behind.
  • Report any damage to the ground or facilities to CAP immediately.
  • All teams must abide by the rules and regulations of the park/township.

Scoring

  • All scorecard changes must be made on the same day as the game. After that, the scorecard is locked.
  • Any scorecard changes needed after game day must be notified to CAP at cappeoria1@gmail.com.

Vehicle Safety on CAP Ground

⚠️ All drivers entering CAP Ground must follow these rules:
  • STOP your vehicle when a ball is being delivered or play is in progress.
  • Drive slowly at all times within the ground premises.
  • Park at a safe distance from the playing area.
  • Be aware of players, children, and spectators walking around the ground.
