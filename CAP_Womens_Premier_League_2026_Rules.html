1. Tournament Format & Structure

League Stage

  • 3 teams: Powerplay Divas, Panthers, Daring Divas.
  • Double round-robin format — each team plays every other team twice (4 games per team, 6 total league matches).
  • 1 game per week — all games are 10 overs per side.
  • Points: Win = 2 pts, Loss = 0 pts, Washout = 1 pt each, Tie = 1 pt each.

Knockout Stage

  • Top 2 teams qualify for the Final.
  • If points are tied, Net Run Rate (NRR) determines standings.

Season Start

  • The CAP Women's Premier League 2026 begins June 13, 2026.

Minimum Players & Toss

  • A team must have a minimum of 7 players on the ground to start the game.
  • Toss time is 10 minutes before the scheduled start.
  • If a team does not have 7 players for the toss, they forfeit the toss.
  • No additional players allowed after 20 minutes from game start.

3. Ground & Playing Area

🏏 Key Difference from Men's Cricket:

Women's games are played on 3/4 of the pitch. The 30-yard circle serves as the boundary.

Playing Area

  • The playing area is 3/4 of the full pitch length.
  • The 30-yard circle is the boundary — any ball crossing the 30-yard circle counts as a boundary (4 runs if along the ground, 6 runs if cleared in the air).
  • Standard cricket stumps and markings apply within the reduced playing area.

Fielding Side

  • 9 players on the ground for the fielding side (not 11).
  • All 9 fielders must remain within the 30-yard circle (boundary) at all times during play.

4. Game Rules

General Rules

  • A new ball shall be used at the start of each innings.
  • No LBW — LBW dismissals are not applicable in the Women's Premier League.
  • Leg-bye runs are allowed as per ICC rules.
  • Batters may use batting gloves.
  • 5-minute break between innings.
Summary of Key Differences from Men's Rules:
  • 3/4 pitch — 30-yard circle is the boundary
  • 9 players fielding (not 11)
  • 10 overs per side
  • No LBW
  • Max 2 overs per bowler

Wide Ball

  • Offside: If any part of the ball is inside the wide line, it is not a wide. The ball must be entirely outside the wide guideline.
  • Leg-side: Any ball passing outside the leg stumps is a wide.
  • Above the head: A ball that bounces and goes above head height is called a wide.

No Ball

  • Full toss above waist height = no ball.
  • Bouncers: Only 1 bouncer per over is allowed. A bouncer is any delivery that rises above shoulder height. Second bouncer in the same over = no ball.
  • All foot-fault no balls apply.
All no balls result in a free hit for the next delivery.

Other Rules

  • Run Out / Stumping: The fielder must hit the stumps with the ball. If stumps are already broken, the fielder must uproot a stump while holding the ball.
  • Retired Hurt: A batsman who is genuinely injured may retire hurt and return later (approved by umpire and opposition captain).
  • Retired Out: A batsman who voluntarily retires cannot return to bat.
  • No substitute runners — not allowed under any circumstances.

Tied Games — Super Over

  1. The team batting second in the main match bats first in the Super Over.
  2. Each side nominates 3 batsmen. Loss of 2 wickets ends the Super Over.
  3. If the Super Over is tied, another Super Over is played until a result is achieved.

5. Bowling & Fielding Rules

Bowling

  • Maximum 2 overs per bowler (10 overs ÷ minimum 5 bowlers).
  • Chucking rules apply — elbow extension exceeding 15 degrees is illegal.

Fielding

  • 9 players on the fielding side at all times.
  • All fielders must remain within the 30-yard circle (the boundary).
  • No fielding restrictions (no powerplay overs) — all 9 fielders can be placed anywhere within the boundary.

6. Umpires & Umpiring Duties

Who Umpires?

  • CAP will assign umpires for Women's Premier League games.
  • The umpire's decision is final.

Recording

  • The batting team must provide a leg umpire who records the match on phone at all times.
  • If the batting team fails to record, any disputed decision favors the bowling team.

7. Delays, Rain & Rescheduling

Game Rescheduling

📋 Rescheduling Policy:

Teams can reschedule a game by reaching out directly to the opposing team's captain. Games can be rescheduled to any weekday that both teams agree upon.

⚠️ Both captains must notify CAP of the rescheduled date.

Weather / Rain Delays

  • Captains and umpires will decide on playing conditions during rain.
  • If both teams cannot play at least 5 overs each, the game is a washout.
  • Washed-out games can be rescheduled to any weekday agreed upon by both captains.
  • If a rescheduled game is also washed out, it becomes split points (1 point each).

Late Arrival

  • 10 minutes late: Team loses 1 over.
  • Every 5 minutes after: 1 additional over deducted.
  • 20 minutes late: Game forfeited if minimum players not present.

9. Practice Booking

Women's teams have dedicated practice slots at Mossville on Monday and Friday evenings.

How to Book

  1. Log in at cap-practice-booking.web.app.
  2. Select the Women's Cricket tab.
  3. Browse available Monday/Friday slots and click Book.
  4. Select your team and confirm.
