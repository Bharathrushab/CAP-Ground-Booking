1. Tournament Format & Structure

League Stage

  • 18 teams divided into 2 groups of 9 (Group A & Group B).
  • Each team plays 8 league matches.
  • All league-stage games are 15 overs per side.
  • Points: Win = 2 pts, Loss = 0 pts, Washout = 1 pt each, Tie = 1 pt each.

Division Split

DivisionQualificationTeamsOvers
Division 1Top 4 from each group8 teams20 overs
Division 2Bottom 5 from each group10 teams15 overs

Division 1 — Cross-Group Format

  • Teams play 3 cross-group matches.
  • A1 plays B2, B3, B4  |  A2 plays B1, B3, B4  |  A3 plays B1, B2, B4  |  A4 plays B1, B2, B3
  • Division 1 standings are independent — based on Division 1 games only.

Division 2 — Cross-Group Format

  • Teams play 3 opponents from the opposite group (randomly drawn from the 5 teams in the other group).
  • Division 2 standings are independent — based on Division 2 games only.

Knockout Stage — Super Groups

  • Super Group 1 (Division 1): Combined points table across both groups for Division 1 games only. Top 4 qualify.
  • Super Group 2 (Division 2): Combined points table across both groups for Division 2 games only. Top 4 qualify.
  • Teams ranked by points, then NRR (independent of league stage results).
  • Semi-Final 1: 1st vs 4th
  • Semi-Final 2: 2nd vs 3rd
  • Final: Winners of Semi-Finals

Minimum Players & Toss

  • A team must have a minimum of 7 players on the ground to start the game.
  • Toss time is 10 minutes before the scheduled start. Playing XI must be updated in CricClubs before the toss.
  • If a team does not have 7 players for the toss, they forfeit the toss. If both teams don't have enough, the first team to reach 7 wins the toss.
  • No additional players allowed after 30 minutes from game start. No excuses.

Late Arrival Penalties

Example: If the game is scheduled at 10:00 AM, the team must have 7 players on the ground and be ready to start.

10:10 AM (10 min late): Team loses 1 over.
Every 5 minutes after that: 1 additional over deducted (10:15 = 2 overs, 10:20 = 3 overs, etc.).
30 minutes late: If team still does not have minimum players, game is forfeited.

⚠️ Umpires MUST enforce this rule strictly. One delayed game must NOT affect the next scheduled game.

3. Player Eligibility & Registration

Minimum Games for Playoffs

A player must have played a minimum of 5 games during the league and division stages to be eligible for knockout matches (semi-finals and finals).

Player Identity & Photos

  • Every player must have a clear profile picture uploaded in the CricClubs app. The face must be clearly recognizable.
  • If a player not on the roster plays under a different player's name, the team has 1 week to update the roster and correct the scorecard in CricClubs.
  • This is only allowed once per team for the entire tournament.
  • If it happens a second time, the unregistered player is not allowed to play and the team must play with 10 players (or fewer if more unregistered players are involved).
  • Umpires are authorized to check player identity using a photo ID during any game, especially during playoffs.
⚠️ One chance only. First time — update the roster within a week. Second time — the player is out and the team plays short.

Waiver Form

  • A waiver form is required for all under-18 players. The waiver must be signed by a parent or guardian before the start of the game.
  • Under-18 players without a signed waiver are not eligible to play.

Player Transfers

No player transfers are allowed. A player must not have played even a single game for another team.

If a player wishes to switch teams, they must email CAP at cappeoria1@gmail.com before playing any game. Once a player has appeared in any match for a team, they cannot transfer.

Roster Changes

A player who has already played a game cannot be removed from the roster to make room for a new player. If the roster is full and the team wants to add someone, they must free a spot by removing a player who has not yet played.

Registration Fees

  • Tournament registration fee: $500 per team.
  • Payment method: Chase QuickPay / Zelle (preferred) to cappeoria1@gmail.com.
  • Checks should be made payable to Cricket Association of Peoria.
⚠️ Late Payment Penalties:

Paid after Week 1 of games starting: additional $50 fine.
Paid after Week 2 of games starting: additional $100 fine.
Not paid by Week 2: Team is excluded from the tournament. No exceptions.

4. Game Rules

Match Format

StageOversMax Overs per BowlerPlayers

General Rules

  • A new ball shall be used at the start of each innings.
  • Each team must bring at least 1 new ball to their game.
  • LBW is applicable — standard ICC LBW rules apply.
  • Leg-bye runs are allowed as per ICC rules.
  • Batters may use batting gloves.
  • 10-minute break between innings.
  • Optional 5-minute mid-innings break if required, at the fielding side's discretion.

LBW — ICC Guidelines for Umpires

For a batsman to be given out LBW, all of the following conditions must be met:

  1. The ball must not pitch outside leg stump (if it pitches outside leg, it is not out regardless).
  2. The ball must strike the batsman's pad (or body) in line with the stumps, or — if the batsman is not offering a shot — the ball may strike outside the line of off stump.
  3. The ball must be going on to hit the stumps in the umpire's judgment (height, direction, and trajectory).

Key points:

  • If the batsman plays a shot and is hit outside the line of off stump → not out.
  • If the batsman does not play a shot, impact outside off stump can still be given out if the ball would have hit the stumps.
  • The ball must not be bouncing too high to hit the stumps — the umpire can review the recording if required to assess the height.

Wide Ball

  • Offside: If any part of the ball is inside the wide line, it is not a wide. The ball must be entirely outside the wide guideline to be called wide.
  • Leg-side: Any ball passing outside the leg stumps is a wide.
  • If a batsman moves across the stumps before the ball is delivered, the wide line moves with the batsman — as per ICC rules.
  • Not a wide if the striker moves and causes the ball to pass wide, or brings the ball within reach of a normal cricket stroke.
  • Above the head: A ball that bounces and goes above head height is called a wide (not a no ball).

No Ball

  • Ball pitching outside the turf before the batting-end wickets (striker's end) = no ball. If the ball pitches outside the turf after passing the batting-end wickets, it may be called a wide following wide guidelines.
  • Full toss above waist height = no ball.
  • Bouncers: Only 1 bouncer per over is allowed. A bouncer is any delivery that rises above shoulder height of the batsman. The first bouncer above shoulder height in an over is a warning. The second bouncer above shoulder height in the same over is a no ball.
  • All foot-fault no balls. Field cannot be changed if bowling to the same batsman.
  • If the bowler hits the stump during delivery = no ball (only if the ball is delivered).
All no balls result in a free hit for the next delivery.

Other Rules

  • Run Out / Stumping — No Bails: Since this format uses stumps without bails, the fielder must hit the stumps with the ball to effect a run out or stumping. If the stumps have already been broken (e.g., by a previous throw or by the bowler's delivery), the fielder must uproot a stump from the ground while holding the ball to complete the dismissal. If all stumps have been uprooted, one stump must be re-planted into the ground and then uprooted again with the ball in hand to complete the dismissal.
  • Retired Hurt: A batsman who is genuinely injured may retire hurt and can return to bat later when fit. Retired hurt must be approved by the umpire and the opposition captain.
  • Retired Out: A batsman who voluntarily retires (not injured) is considered retired out and cannot return to bat.
  • Fielding at Boundary (Catches): If a catch is taken close to the boundary, the fielder must hold their position for the umpire to inspect. If the fielder moves, the umpire will make the judgment call.
  • Fielding at Boundary (4s/6s): For boundary calls, the fielder's call is trusted — the fielder will throw the ball back and signal accordingly. Fielders are expected to be honest.
  • No substitute runners — substitute runners are not allowed under any circumstances.

6. Umpires & Umpiring Duties

6.1 — Who Umpires?

RoleWhoResponsibility
Main Umpire (bowler's end) Player from the assigned team (per schedule) Makes all on-field decisions. Decision is final.
Leg Umpire (leg side) Player from the batting team Records the match on phone at all times. Hands footage to main umpire when requested.
  • The leg umpire's only role is to record and hand over footage. No player or leg umpire shall discuss, argue, or influence the main umpire's decision.
  • If the batting team's leg umpire fails to record, any disputed decision will favor the bowling team.

6.2 — Umpire Arrival

  • Arrive approximately 10 minutes before the scheduled start for field checks and toss.

6.3 — Main Umpire Duties (Before the Game)

  • Check pitch, ground, and ball conditions.
  • Ensure Playing XI is published before the toss.
  • Authorized to check player identity via photo ID, especially during playoffs.

6.4 — Main Umpire Duties (During the Game)

  • Ensure games start and finish as per the schedule.
  • Remain impartial at all times — all decisions are final.
  • Enforce late arrival penalties strictly — one delayed game must not cascade.
  • Track overs when a fielder leaves the field (fielder cannot bat or bowl for that many overs).
  • Keep time of all breaks and notify captains when to resume.

6.5 — Main Umpire Duties (After the Game)

  • Select Man of the Match.

6.6 — What Can Be Checked via Recording

The leg umpire's recording may only be reviewed for:

✅ Allowed Checks❌ NOT Allowed
Run outsSound / audio (edges, nicks, etc.) — no decisions based on sound
No balls (above waist height)
Ball pitching — whether it pitched before or after the batsman
Bouncer height
Height for LBW decisions
Whether a catch was taken cleanly
⚠️ Sound is NOT allowed to be checked. No decisions can be made based on audio/sound from the recording (e.g., edges, nicks).

6.7 — Captain Responsibility: Umpire Notification

📋 Captains must notify CAP of their umpire details:

• Weekend games: Provide umpire name and phone number in the group by Thursday EOD.
• Weekday games: Provide umpire details 2 hours before the match.

⚠️ Do NOT simply "like" or "thumbs up" the post. You MUST type out the umpire's name and phone number as a reply. CAP cannot see thumbs-up reactions as acknowledgment.

📧 You will also receive automatic email notifications from CricClubs for umpiring assignments. Ensure your email notifications are turned on.

7. Delays & Rain Management

Non-Weather Delays

  • 10 minutes after scheduled start: 1 over deducted from the late team.
  • Every 5 minutes after that: 1 additional over deducted.
  • If one team is intentionally delaying, they may be docked 1 point.
  • If a game exceeds the 3-hour window, the teams can come back and finish on a rescheduled day.
  • If your team is unable to start on time due to the previous game overrunning, it is the captain's responsibility to approach the umpire and ensure the ongoing game wraps up. Otherwise your match will have reduced overs.
  • If delays are cascading, umpires and captains must be firm in enforcing the time limit to protect subsequent games.

Game Rescheduling

  • Teams cannot reschedule a game to a later date.
  • A game may only be rescheduled if both teams mutually agree to play on a prior (earlier) date.

Weather / Rain Delays

  • Captains and umpires will decide on playing conditions during rain.
  • If it's already raining, wait for rain to stop, then assess playability.
  • If rain starts after the game begins, evaluate remaining time for a reduced-overs game after rain stops.
  • If both teams cannot play at least 8 overs each, the game is a washout and will be replayed entirely on the rescheduled day.
  • If rain interrupts after at least half of the second innings has been completed, the game will continue from where it was left off on the rescheduled day.
  • Washout rescheduling rules below determine when and where the game is replayed/continued.
  • If a rescheduled game is also washed out, it becomes split points (1 point each).

Washout Rescheduling

Washed-out games are rescheduled to the following week based on which ground they were originally scheduled at:

CAP Ground washouts:
• 1st washout → Monday (following week) at CAP Ground
• 2nd washout → Tuesday at CAP Ground
• Additional washouts continue to next available weekday at CAP Ground

Mossville washouts:
• 1st washout → Monday (following week) at Mossville
• 2nd washout → Tuesday at Mossville
• Additional washouts continue to next available weekday at Mossville

⚠️ If a rescheduled game is also washed out, it becomes split points (1 point each).

Playoffs — Rain Rules

  • Division 1 and Division 2 (Super Group) games follow the same washout and rescheduling rules as league games — they can be washed out and result in split points.
  • All semi-final and final games must have a result.
  • If a semi-final or final is not possible within the scheduled window, CAP reserves the right to reschedule the match.

Semi-Final Format

Each Super Group follows the same knockout format:

  • Semi-Final 1: 1st vs 4th
  • Semi-Final 2: 2nd vs 3rd
  • Final: Winners of Semi-Final 1 and Semi-Final 2

Tied Knockout Games — Super Over (ICC Rules)

  1. The team batting second in the main match bats first in the Super Over.
  2. Each side nominates 3 batsmen. Loss of 2 wickets ends the Super Over.
  3. A player can bat and bowl in the Super Over — as per ICC rules.
  4. If the Super Over is tied, another Super Over is played. This continues until a result is achieved — as per ICC rules.

9. Cricket Bat Specifications

⚠️ Captains must carry the bat gauge provided by CAP. Any bat that does not pass through the gauge is not allowed in match play.
  • Every bat used in a match must pass through the CAP-provided bat gauge. If it does not fit, the bat is not permitted.
  • Overall length: max 38 inches (including handle).
  • Blade width: max 4.25 inches.
  • Blade depth: max 2.64 inches.
  • Blade edges: max 1.56 inches.
  • If a player uses a non-compliant bat and it is noticed mid-game, the bat must be replaced immediately. The umpire or any player should report it to CAP.

10. Bowling & Fielding Rules

Over Rate

  • Captains are responsible for maintaining a healthy over rate to ensure game completion within the scheduled time window.

Chucking (Illegal Bowling Action)

  • Defined by ICC: elbow extension exceeding 15 degrees.
  • Batsman may raise concerns with the main umpire.
  • Umpire will observe the bowler's action and may call a no ball if convinced.
  • If a bowler repeatedly chucks, the opposing team should record video evidence and submit it to CAP. CAP will review the footage and decide on appropriate action.

Fielding

  • Any conspicuous movement or shouting by a fielder during the bowler's delivery stride is prohibited. Umpire may declare the delivery a no ball.
  • Absence Penalty: If a fielder leaves the field of play for any reason during the innings, they are penalized for the same number of overs they were absent:
    • Bowling: Upon returning to the field, the player must complete the same number of overs fielding before they are permitted to bowl.
    • Batting: If the penalty overs have not been fully served during the fielding innings (e.g., the player returned late and the innings ended before the penalty was complete), the remaining overs carry over — the player cannot come in to bat until that many overs have been completed in the batting innings.
    The umpires will track the overs of absence. This rule does not apply if the absence is due to an injury sustained during play and approved by the umpire.

12. Ground Logistics

CAP grounds have permanent pitches and boundary markings. There is no home or away team policy — all teams share equal responsibility for the facilities.

Ground Care

  • Set up wickets before the game. Remove them after the last game of the day.
  • Walk around the ground and surrounding areas after the game to ensure no trash is left behind.
  • Report any damage to the ground or facilities to CAP immediately.
  • All teams must abide by the rules and regulations of the park/township.

Equipment

Each team must carry in their kit bag:

  • Wickets — one set per team (bails are not required)
  • Bat gauge (provided by CAP)
  • New ball (minimum 1 new ball per game)

Scoring

  • All scorecard changes must be made on the same day as the game. After that, the scorecard is locked.
  • Any scorecard changes needed after game day must be notified to CAP at cappeoria1@gmail.com.

Vehicle Safety on CAP Ground

⚠️ All drivers entering CAP Ground must follow these rules:
  • STOP your vehicle when a ball is being delivered or play is in progress. Do not drive through the ground area during active play.
  • Drive slowly at all times within the ground premises.
  • Maintain the speed limit on W Dickison Cemetery Rd — go slow, especially when players and spectators are present.
  • Park at a safe distance from the playing area.
  • Be aware of players, children, and spectators walking around the ground.
